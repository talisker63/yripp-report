## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. **Navigate to the YRIPP System**: Go to the YRIPP website
2. **Click "Create Account"**
3. **Fill in your details**:
   - Full name (as you want it to appear on reports)
   - Email address (for login)
   - Strong password (minimum 6 characters)
4. **Or use Google Sign-In**: Click "Sign in with Google" for quick access

### First Login

After creating your account:
1. You'll be automatically logged in
2. You'll see the **Reports Dashboard** - your home base
3. From here you can:
   - View all your reports
   - Create new reports
   - Access settings
   - Get help

## Creating Your First Report

### Starting a New Report

1. **Click "New Report"** on the Reports Dashboard
2. You'll see the **Interview Report Form** with multiple sections
3. Complete the sections in order (though you can jump between them)

### Navigating the Form

- **Section Numbers**: Click to jump to any section
- **Next/Previous Buttons**: Move through sections sequentially
- **Save Draft**: Save your progress at any time
- **Submit Report**: Finalize and submit (only when complete)

### Saving Your Work

**Important**: The form does NOT auto-save!

- Click **"Save Draft"** frequently as you work
- Saved drafts appear in your Reports Dashboard
- You can return to edit drafts anytime before submission

## Managing Reports

### Reports Dashboard

Your dashboard shows all your reports in a table:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Interview Date** | Date of the interview |
| **Independent Person** | Your name |
| **Police Station** | Where the interview took place |
| **Status** | Draft (yellow) or Submitted (green) |
| **Last Updated** | When the report was last modified |
| **Actions** | Edit (drafts) or View (submitted) |

### Filtering Reports

Use the filter buttons to view:
- **All Reports**: See everything
- **Drafts**: Work in progress
- **Submitted**: Completed reports

### Editing a Draft

1. Find the draft in your dashboard
2. Click **"Edit"**
3. Make your changes
4. Click **"Save Draft"** to save
5. When ready, click **"Submit Report"**

### Viewing Submitted Reports

1. Find the submitted report in your dashboard
2. Click **"View"**
3. You can read the report but **cannot edit it**
4. Submitted reports maintain legal integrity

**Note**: Only YRIPP Staff can edit submitted reports (with full audit trail).

## Understanding Report Sections

### Section A: Call Out and Arrival

Document the initial contact and your arrival at the police station.

**Key Information:**
- Your name
- Police station name
- Interview date and times
- Police contact details
- Reason parent/guardian not attending
- Custody duration
- Offence types
- DHHS facility involvement

### Section B Part A: Young Person Information

Collect demographic and initial assessment information.

**Key Information:**
- Young person's preferred first name
- Immediate needs (food, water, medication, etc.)
- Satisfaction with police treatment
- Any injuries observed
- Demographics (age, gender, cultural identity)
- Living arrangements
- ATSI status and VALS contact
- Language needs
- Previous police interview experience

**Privacy Note**: Use preferred first name only, never full names in reports.

### Section C: Independent Person Observations

Your professional observations during the interview.

**Key Information:**
- Opportunity to speak privately with young person
- Explanations provided about your role and interview process
- Young person's understanding
- Appropriate adult presence
- Legal representation
- IP concerns about police treatment
- Medical needs
- Communication issues

### Interview Section

Document what occurred during the interview itself.

**Key Information:**
- Interview start and end times
- Who was present
- Whether interview was recorded
- Actions you witnessed (reading rights, breaks, etc.)
- Concerns raised during interview
- Legal advice received

### Outcome Section

Record the interview outcome and next steps.

**Key Information:**
- Interview outcome (charged, released, etc.)
- Court appearance details if applicable
- Bail hearing information
- Court support program offered

### IP Concerns Section

**Critical Section**: Document any concerns you have about:
- Police treatment
- Young person's wellbeing
- Interview process
- Any issues requiring follow-up

**Be specific**: Include what happened, when, and why you're concerned.

### Section B Part B: Young Person Support Services

Information about support services and referrals.

**Key Information:**
- Current support services
- Additional services needed
- Referrals made
- Action wanted by young person

### Section E: After Interview

Document post-interview activities.

**Key Information:**
- Spoke to sergeant (and about what)
- Property returned
- Accommodation arrangements
- Transportation home
- Whether you saw young person leave
- YRIPP follow-up desired

### Section F: Additional Notes

Any additional observations or information not covered in other sections.

### Office Use Only

Administrative information (completed by YRIPP staff or you if you have details):
- Call ID
- Related call IDs
- Direct referral information
- IRS received details

## Submitting Reports

### When to Submit

Submit your report when:
- All sections are complete
- You've reviewed for accuracy
- You've documented all concerns
- You've saved a final draft

### How to Submit

1. **Review Your Report**: Go through each section
2. **Click "Submit Report"**: At the bottom of any section
3. **Confirm Submission**: A popup will ask you to confirm
4. **Report is Finalized**: Status changes to "Submitted"

### After Submission

**Important Rules:**
- ✅ You CAN view submitted reports anytime
- ❌ You CANNOT edit submitted reports
- ✅ YRIPP Staff can make corrections if needed
- ✅ All staff edits are tracked with reasons

### Why Can't I Edit Submitted Reports?

Interview reports are legal documents. Once submitted, they must remain unchanged to maintain their integrity and credibility in legal proceedings. If corrections are needed, YRIPP Staff will make them with a full audit trail showing who, when, why, and what was changed.

## Account Settings

### Accessing Settings

Click the **⚙️ Settings** icon in the header (next to your name).

### What You Can Update

1. **Full Name**: Your display name in reports (updates immediately)
2. **Phone Number**: Optional contact number (updates immediately)
3. **Email Address**: Your login email (requires current password verification, signs you out after change)

### Changing Your Password

**Option 1: Password Reset (Recommended)**
1. Sign out
2. Click "Forgot password?" on the login page
3. Enter your email address
4. Check your email for a password reset link
5. Click the link and follow instructions to set a new password

**Option 2: If You Know Your Current Password**
1. Sign out
2. Sign back in with your current password
3. Go to Settings (⚙️ icon)
4. Use the password reset link if available

**Note**: Password reset links expire after 1 hour for security reasons.

### Security Tips

- Use a strong, unique password
- Don't share your login credentials
- Sign out when using shared computers
- Consider using Google Sign-In for added security

## Tips and Best Practices

### Creating Reports

1. **Start Early**: Begin the report as soon as possible after the interview while details are fresh
2. **Save Often**: Click "Save Draft" after completing each section
3. **Be Specific**: Include dates, times, names, and specific actions
4. **Document Concerns**: Don't hesitate to note concerns - that's your job
5. **Use Plain Language**: Write clearly and avoid jargon
6. **Review Before Submitting**: Check for completeness and accuracy

### Writing Effective Reports

**DO:**
- ✅ Use factual, objective language
- ✅ Note what you directly observed
- ✅ Include relevant details (times, who was present, what was said)
- ✅ Document your concerns clearly
- ✅ Use the young person's preferred name

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Include assumptions or speculation
- ❌ Use the young person's full name
- ❌ Include overly personal details unless relevant
- ❌ Make legal judgments (that's for lawyers)
- ❌ Rush through sections

### Managing Your Reports

1. **Use Filters**: Find specific reports quickly using filter buttons
2. **Complete Drafts Promptly**: Don't let drafts pile up
3. **Check Regularly**: Review your submitted reports occasionally
4. **Track Your Hours**: Note call time and left station time accurately

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Can't Sign In
- Check your email and password are correct
- Try the "Forgot password?" link
- Clear your browser cache and cookies
- Try a different browser

#### Draft Not Saving
- Check your internet connection
- Click "Save Draft" button explicitly
- Look for error messages
- Try refreshing the page (but you may lose unsaved work)

#### Can't Find a Report
- Check which filter is active (All/Drafts/Submitted)
- Look at the Interview Date column
- Remember: You can only see YOUR reports (not other IPs' reports)

#### Report Shows "Loading..."
- Check your internet connection
- Wait a few moments (large reports take time to load)
- Refresh the page
- Try signing out and back in

### Browser Compatibility

YRIPP works best on:
- ✅ Google Chrome (latest version)
- ✅ Microsoft Edge (latest version)
- ✅ Firefox (latest version)
- ✅ Safari (latest version on Mac/iOS)

**Requirements:**
- JavaScript must be enabled
- Cookies must be enabled
- Internet connection required

### Mobile Devices

The system works on phones and tablets:
- Use landscape orientation on phones for easier viewing
- All features available on mobile
- Consider using a tablet or computer for report creation

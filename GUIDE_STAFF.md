## Staff Role Overview

### Your Responsibilities

As a YRIPP Staff member, you have elevated privileges and important responsibilities:

**You Can:**
- ✅ View ALL interview reports from all Independent Persons
- ✅ View both draft and submitted reports
- ✅ Edit ANY report, including submitted reports
- ✅ Add clarifications or corrections as needed
- ✅ View complete audit trail of all edits
- ✅ Create your own reports (if you also have IP role)

**You Must:**
- 🔸 Provide a clear reason for every edit you make
- 🔸 Maintain the integrity of original observations
- 🔸 Only make necessary changes
- 🔸 Respect the confidentiality of sensitive information
- 🔸 Document all changes in the audit trail

### Understanding User Roles

The YRIPP system has three roles:

1. **User (Independent Person)**: Creates and manages own reports
2. **Staff**: Reviews and edits all reports (YOU)
3. **Admin**: Manages user accounts (may also be you)

**Note**: Users can have multiple roles. If you're also an Admin, you'll see an "Admin" button in the header.

## Editing Reports

### When to Edit Reports

Edit reports when:
- ✅ Factual errors need correction
- ✅ Additional information must be added
- ✅ IP has requested an update
- ✅ Administrative corrections are needed
- ✅ Clarification is required for legal purposes

**Do NOT edit to:**
- ❌ Change the IP's observations or opinions
- ❌ Remove legitimate concerns raised
- ❌ Make subjective changes
- ❌ Without a documented reason

### How to Edit a Report

1. **Find the Report**: In Reports Dashboard
2. **Click "View" or "Edit"**: Opens the report
3. **Make Your Changes**: Edit any section as needed
4. **Click "Save Draft"**: (Not "Submit" - report is already submitted)
5. **Edit Reason Modal Appears**:
   - Enter a clear, detailed reason for your edit
   - Explain what was changed and why
   - Who requested it (if applicable)
6. **Click "Save"**: Changes are saved with audit entry

### Edit Reason Guidelines

Your edit reason should include:

**What**: What was changed?
**Why**: Why was it changed?
**Who**: Who requested it (if applicable)?
**When**: When was the request made (if applicable)?

**Good Examples:**

✅ *"Updated police station name from 'Melbourne Central' to 'Melbourne CBD' per IP correction request on 11/01/2026."*

✅ *"Added missing outcome information provided by IP via email on 10/01/2026. Young person was released on bail."*

✅ *"Corrected interview time from 14:30 to 15:30 to match police records. Confirmed with IP John Smith on 12/01/2026."*

✅ *"Clarified IP concerns section based on follow-up conversation with IP. Original concerns remain unchanged, added context about police response."*

**Bad Examples:**

❌ *"Fixed error"* - Too vague, doesn't explain what or why

❌ *"Updated"* - No context about what changed

❌ *"As requested"* - Doesn't say what was requested or by whom

❌ *"Corrected typo"* - Doesn't specify what typo or where

### Editing Submitted vs Draft Reports

**Submitted Reports:**
- Always require edit reason
- Generate audit trail entry
- Cannot be edited by original IP

**Draft Reports:**
- If you're NOT the owner, still requires edit reason
- If you ARE the owner, no edit reason needed

## Audit Trail Management

### What is the Audit Trail?

Every time a staff member edits a submitted report, a permanent record is created showing:
- **Who** made the edit (name and role)
- **When** the edit was made (date and time)
- **Why** the edit was made (edit reason)

### Viewing Audit Trail

**In Reports Dashboard:**
- Look at the "Edit History" column
- Click on reports with edits to see full history

**In Report View:**
- Open any report that has been edited
- Yellow banner at top shows complete edit history
- Each edit shows editor name, date/time, and reason

**Example:**
```
Report has been edited by staff:
• Edited by: Jane Smith (Staff) on 11/01/2026 at 14:30
  Reason: Corrected police station name per IP request
  
• Edited by: John Doe (Staff) on 10/01/2026 at 09:15
  Reason: Added missing outcome information from IRS
```

### Why Audit Trails Matter

1. **Legal Compliance**: Courts may require documentation of changes
2. **Transparency**: IPs can see what was changed and why
3. **Quality Assurance**: Track patterns in errors or corrections
4. **Accountability**: Staff actions are permanently recorded

### Audit Trail Best Practices

- Write clear, professional edit reasons
- Be specific about what changed
- Reference sources (emails, phone calls, documents)
- Include dates when referencing communications
- Use complete sentences
- Proofread your edit reason before saving

## Best Practices

### Report Review Workflow

1. **Prioritize New Submissions**: Review reports shortly after submission
2. **Check Completeness**: Ensure all sections are filled out
3. **Verify Key Details**: 
   - Times and dates
   - Names and locations
   - Concerns documented
4. **Contact IP if Needed**: For clarifications or missing info
5. **Make Necessary Edits**: With proper documentation
6. **Follow Up on Concerns**: Address any IP concerns raised

### Quality Assurance

Look for:
- ✅ Completeness (all sections filled)
- ✅ Clarity (observations clearly described)
- ✅ Accuracy (times, dates, names correct)
- ✅ Concerns documented (if applicable)
- ✅ Professional language
- ✅ Privacy maintained (no full names)

### Communication with IPs

**When to Contact an IP:**
- Missing critical information
- Need clarification on observations
- Before making significant changes
- Following up on concerns raised

**How to Contact:**
- Email (preferred - creates record)
- Phone (follow up with email summary)
- Through YRIPP administration

**Document All Contact:**
- Note in edit reason if change is based on IP communication
- Include date and method of contact

### Handling Concerns

When reports document concerns about police treatment:

1. **Take Seriously**: Every concern deserves attention
2. **Verify Details**: Ensure concern is clearly documented
3. **Follow Protocol**: Use organizational procedures for escalation
4. **Document Action**: Note what steps were taken
5. **Follow Up**: Ensure resolution or appropriate escalation

### Data Privacy and Security

- 🔒 Never discuss report contents outside secure channels
- 🔒 Don't access reports without legitimate reason
- 🔒 Always sign out when finished
- 🔒 Don't share your credentials
- 🔒 Be mindful when viewing reports in public spaces

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Can't See All Reports
- Confirm you have Staff role (check header badge)
- Try refreshing the page
- Sign out and sign back in
- Contact Admin if role is missing

#### Edit Reason Modal Won't Save
- Ensure you've entered text in the reason field
- Check for error messages
- Try refreshing and re-entering
- Contact support if persistent

#### Report Won't Load
- Check internet connection
- Clear browser cache
- Try different browser
- Report may be corrupted - contact support

#### Changes Not Appearing
- Ensure you clicked "Save"
- Check you entered edit reason
- Refresh the page
- Look for error messages

### Browser Requirements

Best performance on:
- Chrome (latest version)
- Edge (latest version)  
- Firefox (latest version)
- Safari (latest on Mac/iOS)

**Requirements:**
- JavaScript enabled
- Cookies enabled
- Internet connection

## Appendix: Staff Checklist

### When Reviewing a Report

- [ ] All sections completed
- [ ] Times and dates accurate
- [ ] Police station and officers identified
- [ ] Young person's preferred name used (no full names)
- [ ] Immediate needs addressed
- [ ] Private conversation opportunity documented
- [ ] Interview details recorded
- [ ] Outcome documented
- [ ] Concerns noted (if any)
- [ ] Support services documented
- [ ] Post-interview activities recorded

### When Making Edits

- [ ] Edit is necessary
- [ ] You have clear reason for edit
- [ ] Edit maintains original intent
- [ ] You've drafted edit reason
- [ ] Edit reason includes what/why/who
- [ ] Edit reason references source if applicable

### Quality Indicators

**Green Flags** (Good Reports):
- ✅ Complete and detailed
- ✅ Clear timeline
- ✅ Specific observations
- ✅ Concerns clearly articulated
- ✅ Professional language
- ✅ Privacy maintained

**Yellow Flags** (May Need Follow-up):
- ⚠️ Missing sections
- ⚠️ Vague descriptions
- ⚠️ Inconsistent times
- ⚠️ Unclear concerns
- ⚠️ May need IP clarification

**Red Flags** (Immediate Attention):
- 🚩 Serious concerns documented
- 🚩 Potential rights violations
- 🚩 Medical issues
- 🚩 Safety concerns
- 🚩 Requires immediate follow-up
